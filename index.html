01 · Problem

The most expensive step in fashion is not design — it is translation

Manual Cost
7 days

~1,500 BOM files per season, per brand — about six weeks of a full-time development team's work.

Error Propagation
30%+

Visible losses from spec ambiguity: wrong fabric, rework, late delivery, lost sales — compounding downstream.

Language Barrier
8+

The same craft term is never fully reconciled across Chinese, English, and Italian supply chains.

This is not a creative problem. It is a translation problem — and translation is computable.
